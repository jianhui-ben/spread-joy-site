Pints for a Purpose

What happens when you fill a brewery with generous hearts, good humans, and incredible raffle prizes? A whole lot of joy — and a community showing up for local families.

Pints for a Purpose is our annual fundraiser and one of our favorite ways to bring people together. Good, kind, accepting people coming together to make a difference is contagious. So is joy, and we love seeing it ripple beyond the event.

At the heart of it is raising funds to support local families of individuals with disabilities. Every gift earns raffle tickets, and you choose which prizes you want to try to win. Every person matters. Every gift matters.
