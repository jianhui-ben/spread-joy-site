## What Squarespace has now

The Events page exists at `/events` and is in Main Navigation. In the editor on 2026-10-17 it was an **empty page** (“Create a beautiful page by adding and combining sections.”). There is no date, place, or registration link on the site.

## How to make the page

Use native Squarespace sections, not a Code Block. The copy below is from the owner’s brainstorm doc (`assets/Website Brainstorm Ideas.docx`), not from a published event listing.

1. Open the existing Events page (`/events`).
2. Section 1: image (`assets/img/socks-circle.jpg`, already uploaded) beside the Pints copy. Button label **Give**, linking to `https://givebutter.com/SpreadJoyCollective`.
3. Leave the yellow line `[TBD: event date, place, and how to join]` until the founder supplies it. Do not invent a date.
4. Section 2, different background: the Socks note, with `[TBD: next sock drive date, place, and what to bring]`.
5. `LINK_EVENTS` in `home-page/image-urls.json` is `/events`.

## Copy: Pints for a Purpose

What happens when you fill a brewery with generous hearts, good humans, and incredible raffle prizes? A whole lot of joy — and a community showing up for local families.

Pints for a Purpose is our annual fundraiser and one of our favorite ways to bring people together. Good, kind, accepting people coming together to make a difference is contagious. So is joy, and we love seeing it ripple beyond the event.

At the heart of it is raising funds to support local families of individuals with disabilities. Every gift earns raffle tickets, and you choose which prizes you want to try to win. Every person matters. Every gift matters.
